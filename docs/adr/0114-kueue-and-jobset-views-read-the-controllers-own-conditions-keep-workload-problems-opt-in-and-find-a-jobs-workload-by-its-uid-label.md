# ADR-0114: Kueue and JobSet views read the controllers' own conditions, keep Workload problems opt-in and find a job's Workload by its uid label

## Context

#246 asked for Kueue (`kueue.x-k8s.io`: ClusterQueue, LocalQueue, ResourceFlavor, Workload, AdmissionCheck, Cohort, WorkloadPriorityClass) and #247 for JobSet (`jobset.x-k8s.io/v1alpha2`), in the style of the other CRD projects (ADR-0097, 0100, 0101): discovered sidebar sections, a status column, details that explain a failure from the objects' own status, problems, fixtures, all 14 languages and axe tests, read-only. Both are batch tools and meet on the same objects: Kueue queues a JobSet through a Workload. Kueue serves `v1beta1` up to v0.14 and `v1beta2` since (main serves `v1beta2` only); JobSet serves `v1alpha2`. Sources read: `apis/kueue/v1beta1|v1beta2/*_types.go`, `pkg/scheduler/scheduler.go`, `pkg/controller/core/{workload,localqueue}_controller.go`, `pkg/workload/{workload.go,evict/evict.go}`, `pkg/controller/constants/constants.go` at v0.20.3 and v0.14.0; `api/jobset/v1alpha2/jobset_types.go`, `pkg/constants/constants.go`, `pkg/controllers/failure_policy.go` at v0.13.0.

## Decision

- **Read-only, frontend only.** `src/features/kueue/` is the discovered section `kueue` with the seven kinds (ClusterQueue, ResourceFlavor, Cohort, AdmissionCheck and WorkloadPriorityClass cluster-scoped; LocalQueue and Workload namespaced); `src/features/jobset/` the discovered section `jobset` with JobSet. No Rust command was added, nothing is written; the kinds offer the generic Edit and Delete only. Strings are the namespaces `kueue` and `jobset`.
- **Both Kueue versions by field.** Discovery serves one version, so `model.ts` reads the few renamed fields both ways: a ClusterQueue's cohort (`cohortName` / `cohort`), a LocalQueue's usage (`flavorsUsage` / `flavorUsage`), a Workload's priority class (`priorityClassRef.name` / `priorityClassName`), admission checks (`admissionChecksStrategy` / `admissionChecks`). Links name `v1beta2`; opening resolves the served version.
- **A queue's state is its `Active` condition**: True active; False `Stopped` (the user's `stopPolicy`) and `Terminating` are not faults; any other False reason (`FlavorNotFound`, `AdmissionCheckNotFound`, `CohortCycleDetected`, …) is inactive and its message is shown. A ClusterQueue without a `namespaceSelector` selects no namespace (the API's documented default), an empty one selects all.
- **Quota against usage per flavor and resource**: each `resourceGroups[].flavors[].resources[]` row beside `status.flavorsReservation` (reserved, with its `borrowed` part and the share of the nominal quota — over 100% while borrowing) and `status.flavorsUsage` (admitted), with the borrowing and lending limits. A Cohort shows its own quotas without usage.
- **A Workload's state from its conditions**, in Kueue's order: Finished (Failed reason → failed), `spec.active: false` → deactivated, Admitted → admitted, Evicted without a reservation → evicted, QuotaReserved True → waiting for admission checks, QuotaReserved False → pending, or inadmissible when the reason is `Inadmissible` or `Misconfigured` and the message does not say the queue is stopped or inactive (that is the queue's state, told through it). Why it waits is the QuotaReserved=False message the scheduler writes ("couldn't assign flavors to pod set main: insufficient unused quota for cpu in flavor …"), shown as Kueue wrote it. The owner job is the controller owner reference, else the composable-job annotations `kueue.x-k8s.io/job-owner-gvk` (`gvk.String()`) and `job-owner-name`. Pod sets show the requests (`status.resourceRequests`) and the assigned flavors (`status.admission.podSetAssignments`); evictions come from `status.schedulingStats.evictions` (else `requeueState.count`).
- **A queue's waiting Workloads** come from a row tap (`acquireRows`) on the problems store's cluster-wide Workload watch, so the details open no second Workload watch; the LocalQueues come from the resource map's shared watches (all namespaces, few objects). A ClusterQueue lists the waiting Workloads of the LocalQueues pointing at it, oldest first, at most 50, with how long and why; a LocalQueue its own.
- **A job's "Queue" section** (a details section on the kinds Kueue's integrations queue: Job, JobSet, RayJob, RayCluster, the Kubeflow jobs, TrainJob, LeaderWorkerSet, AppWrapper) finds its Workload by the label Kueue puts on it, `kueue.x-k8s.io/job-uid=<the job's uid>`, selected by the API server in the job's namespace, kept only when its owner references name that uid; a job naming a prebuilt Workload (`kueue.x-k8s.io/prebuilt-workload-name`) also gets that one by name. Nothing is watched where Workloads are not served; a job without the queue label shows the section only when a Workload exists (`manageJobsWithoutQueueName`).
- **Kueue problems are two categories.** `kueue` (on by default): a ClusterQueue inactive for another reason than a stop, and a LocalQueue whose ClusterQueue does not exist (`ClusterQueueDoesNotExist`; a LocalQueue of an inactive ClusterQueue is told through it), after 2 minutes; sources ClusterQueue and LocalQueue (≤ 5000). `kueueWorkloads` (opt-in, a toggle on the Problems page): a Workload waiting for quota (`Pending`, `WaitingForQuota`, `NoMatchingFlavor`, `ExceedsMaxQuota`, `TopologyPlacementFailed`, `WaitingForPreemptedWorkloads`) 30 minutes after QuotaReserved turned False; inadmissible after 5 minutes; evicted 3 times or more (while evicted, then an hour after the last eviction); deactivated by Kueue for a failure (`RequeuingLimitExceeded`, `MaximumExecutionTimeExceeded`, a rejected admission check — not a deactivation the user made). Its source is every Workload of the cluster, capped at 5000.
- **JobSet state from its conditions and `terminalState`**: Completed, Failed, Suspended (or `spec.suspend`), `RestartingJobSet`, `StartupPolicyInProgress`, running when a replicated job has active or ready Jobs, else pending. Restarts that count are `restartsCountTowardsMax` plus the Jobs' `jobRestartsCountTowardsMax` (as `totalRestartsCountTowardsMax`), every restart before v0.6; without a failure policy there is no limit. The failed Job is read from the Failed message's suffix "(first failed job: <name>)" (`messageWithFirstFailedJob`), linked, and its own Failed condition reason and message shown from the JobSet's Jobs.
- **A JobSet's Jobs and pods** are listed while its details are open by the label `jobset.sigs.k8s.io/jobset-name=<name>` in its namespace (the API server's selector), each list at most 500 objects; pods are shown not-running first, at most 50.
- **JobSet problems** (category `jobset`, on by default; JobSets capped at 5000): a failed JobSet with the controller's reason and message, for a day after the Failed condition's time (as Argo Workflows); and, while it runs, restarts that leave at most one before `maxRestarts`.
- **Agents get nothing new**: `kueue`, `kueueWorkloads` and `jobset` are in `NEVER_FOR_AGENTS` (`src-tauri/src/mcp/diagnose_answers.rs`, a test there); no MCP tool or extension method was added.

## Why

- The conditions are the controllers' own verdicts; recomputing whether a Workload fits a quota would need the scheduler's whole state (cohorts, preemption, fair sharing, topology) and would be wrong where it differs.
- Workload objects carry their jobs' pod templates, and a batch cluster queues thousands: a cluster-wide background list of them is the cost for which `traefik`, `reflector` and `sealedSecrets` are opt-in (ADR-0108, 0110, 0109 amended). The queues are few, and an inactive ClusterQueue stops every job behind it: an incident worth showing by default.
- The job-uid label lets the API server pick one job's Workload; a name rule (`job-<name>-<hash>`) would need the hash Kueue computes and differs per integration.
- A failed JobSet's own message names its first failed Job, so no guess over the Jobs is needed; that Job's condition gives the reason people look for (`BackoffLimitExceeded`, `PodFailurePolicy`).

## Consequences

- With `kueueWorkloads` off, the Kueue section's counter shows only queue problems; a ClusterQueue's waiting list still works while its details are open (the tap holds the Workload watch only then).
- More than 5000 Workloads: the waiting lists say they may be incomplete; the problems source says it is truncated.
- A job whose Workload Kueue made before the job-uid label existed (very old Kueue) shows "no Workload yet"; its Workload is still in the Workloads table.
- JobSets older than v0.6 count every restart toward the limit, as they did then.
